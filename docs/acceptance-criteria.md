# Acceptance Criteria Evidence Mapping

This guide connects the university portal's acceptance criteria and evaluation areas to the delivered behaviour, the screens where it can be reviewed, and the implementation and verification evidence. It covers student, lecturer, department administrator/HOD, and Super Admin workflows. The department administrator is called HOD in the supplied demo; access is determined by scoped privileges.

Start with the [hosted student portal](https://uemp-student.onrender.com/sign-in) or [hosted staff portal](https://uemp-admin.onrender.com/sign-in), the [demo accounts](../README.md#demo-access-and-walkthrough), and the [reviewer walkthrough](reviewer-guide.md). Local setup, hosted URLs, Swagger credentials, and the technology rationale are in the [main README](../README.md). Source and test links below are evidence locations; use the [verification instructions](development.md#verification) to run the suites against a disposable database.

## Role responsibilities and restrictions

| Persona                      | What the delivered app supports                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Boundary to verify                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Student                      | Secure sign-in, permitted profile edits, faculty/department/programme/level/matriculation identity, available courses, registration, unit totals, approved grades, academic history, GPA/CGPA, and printable summaries/statements. | A student cannot enroll another student through self-service, modify official placement/marks, view another student's statement, or enter staff workspaces.                                        |
| Lecturer                     | Assigned courses, registered class lists, permanent roster closure, CA/exam drafts, submission, feedback/status, and course performance summaries.                                                                                 | Assignment and department privileges are both required. Complete marks and a closed roster are necessary for submission. A lecturer cannot approve their own work through an additional HOD grant. |
| Department administrator/HOD | Department programmes, curricula, offerings, lecturer assignment/provisioning, student placement, registration oversight/delegation, review, approval/correction, and department reports.                                          | Each privilege is scoped by its own assignment. Department staff cannot administer another department or university-wide settings. Their own results require a different approver.                 |
| Super Admin                  | University dashboard, faculties/departments/programmes, sessions/semesters, course catalogue and offerings, account status/lifecycle, roles/grants, registration oversight, publication oversight, reports, and audit.             | University actions still need their privileges. A university-wide protected Super Admin assignment alone permits the self-approval exception; a custom role label does not.                        |

## Business rules and technical focus points

| Focus point                                            | Implemented rule and supporting documentation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Programme, department, level, and semester eligibility | Registration validates the student's saved/current academic context against curriculum and the offering's owning department. See [registration rules](workflows.md#course-registration) and [academic relationships](database.md).                                                                                                                                      |
| Duplicate prevention and credit limits                 | A student cannot register the same catalogue course twice in one semester, including through separate offerings. The whole selected batch and semester unit total are validated transactionally. See [registration](workflows.md#course-registration).                                                                                                                  |
| Department ownership of shared courses                 | Catalogue definitions can be reused, but each departmental offering owns its lecturer, roster, grading policy, and approvals. A programme-course junction does not grant another department access. See [database design](database.md).                                                                                                                                 |
| Closure and marks locking                              | Roster closure is permanent. No late-registration or roster-reopen endpoint exists. Submitted marks lock until correction/reopening; reopening results never reopens registration. See [result workflow](workflows.md#marks-review-and-publication).                                                                                                                    |
| Independent approval                                   | A lecturer/HOD cannot approve their own taught, entered, or submitted marks. Protected university-wide Super Admin status is the explicit exception, with ordinary approval checks retained. See [authorization](security.md#accounts-roles-and-scopes).                                                                                                                |
| Approved-only publication                              | Approval writes grade awards and group state in one transaction. Reopening withdraws publication and excludes the awards from student results and averages until reapproval. See [publication integrity](security.md#academic-integrity-and-publication).                                                                                                               |
| CA/exam, grades, GPA, and CGPA                         | The initial documented policy uses CA 30 plus exam 70, with a five-point grade scale. Offering policies and enrollment units are pinned. Averages are credit-weighted, repeated attempts count separately, and rounding occurs after summation/division. See the [formula, scale, and worked example](workflows.md#gpa-and-cgpa).                                       |
| Backend permission checks and secure records           | The API validates current account status, privilege scope, assignment, workflow state, and self-approval rules. Token rotation, session revocation, no-store protected responses, and PII redaction are documented in [security](security.md).                                                                                                                          |
| Data validation and friendly failures                  | Request/response DTOs document examples; validators and the exception boundary produce readable messages. Database write wrappers map constraints without exposing SQL or submitted secrets. See [validation and errors](security.md#input-response-and-failure-boundaries).                                                                                            |
| Audit trails and instrumentation                       | Administrative actions and result transitions record semantic events with safe actor/resource IDs and correlation. Critical academic success audits commit with the change. Logs explain outcomes; development/staging do not export traces/metrics. See [audit and observability](architecture.md#audit-and-observability).                                            |
| Responsive interfaces and user feedback                | Shared tokens/components cover phone-to-desktop layouts, light/dark themes, loading/empty/error states, keyboard access, unsaved marks, recovery, and authenticated print views. See the [design system](../frontend/docs/design-system.md), [responsive browser review](../frontend/e2e/ui-review.spec.ts), and [finishing checks](../frontend/e2e/finishing.spec.ts). |
| Reproducible setup and sample data                     | One root `.env` is sufficient for Compose. Its initializer runs migrations and seeds a fresh database before serving traffic; committed seed markers preserve later edits. See [setup](../README.md#run-the-complete-application) and [configuration](development.md#configuration-and-operations).                                                                     |

## Scope and evidence limits

This is an academic-management MVP. School-fee payment gateways, accommodation, admissions screening, biometric attendance, SMS/email gateways, mobile apps, transcript certification, and live integration with external university systems are outside the delivered scope. Printing supplies registration summaries and result statements, not certified transcripts.

The [verification guide](development.md#verification) distinguishes unit/source checks, real PostgreSQL/Redis integration, development browser suites, production-container delivery, and opt-in expiry tests. Tests exercise synthetic disposable data; they do not certify every browser, assistive technology, production load, disaster-recovery procedure, or external infrastructure. The [operational scope](reviewer-guide.md#operational-scope) records deployment boundaries. Existing hosted data can differ from the initial seed after a walkthrough; re-running seeding preserves those changes.
