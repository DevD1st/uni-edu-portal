# Assessment verification

This report compares the implementation with assessment sections 1–11 and Important Candidate Clarifications in the owner's 20 screenshots, plus subsequent approved corrections. Source evidence and executed checks are recorded separately.

The functional assessment scope and approved overrides are implemented. This audit fixed missing student placement labels and verified the correction. External submission handoff remains unverified: hosted URLs, repository access, evaluator credentials and video/live-demo arrangements still need confirmation.

## Scope and approved decisions

- Implement all four workflows. Student and Admin are account types; Lecturer, HOD, and Super Admin are staff grants. Multiple grants can coexist; each privilege is checked against its department scope.
- A lecturer may approve their own submission when their account also has the owning department's approval privilege. This explicitly replaces the original self-approval restriction.
- Programmes can share catalog courses; each department administers its own offering, roster, lecturer, and results. Registration cannot resume after roster closure, including after results are returned or reopened.
- Use `Role.name`; `AdminRole` supplies the role assignment and optional department scope. University programme types and the documented academic level labels follow the approved schema. Account deletion redacts personal data while retaining academic and audit history under internal IDs.
- Default grading is CA 30/exam 70 on a versioned five-point scale. Failures and approved repeat attempts retain their units. Round GPA/CGPA after division; derive CGPA from credit points, not displayed term GPAs.
- Registration summaries and result statements open authenticated print views. The UI print action is the approved replacement for download buttons.
- Keep one Compose file with automatic migrations and demo initialization. Frontends use the HTTP API; PostgreSQL, Redis, and token revocation remain backend infrastructure.

The source conflicts on timing/scope: a 72-hour header, 48-hour exclusion heading, 24-hour single-workflow clarification, and later 72-hour confirmation. The owner approved all workflows. The screenshot deadline is 12 November 2026, 11:55 PM Abuja/Lagos (WAT, GMT+1).
