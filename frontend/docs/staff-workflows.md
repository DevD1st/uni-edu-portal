# Staff portal workflows

The staff portal implements the assessment's lecturer, department administrator, and university administrator workflows. Staff sign in as administrators; the assigned privileges and their department scopes determine the navigation, direct routes, and available actions. Role names are labels. The API rechecks current access and record state on every operation.

## Access and behavior

| Workspace              | Required access                                                                                         | Behavior                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| My teaching            | `teach_course` and the actual offering assignment                                                       | Lists assigned offerings and class rosters. A lecturer can permanently close an enrolled roster.                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Marks                  | `teach_course` and `grade_result`, both covering the owning department, plus the assignment             | Edits draft or returned marks, validates the pinned CA/exam limits, and submits a complete closed roster. Omitted score components remain unchanged; clearing a score removes it.                                                                                                                    |
| Result review          | Any of `review_result`, `approve_result`, or `reopen_result` in the owning department                   | Lists previously submitted groups. Correction, approval/publication, and reopening buttons each require their own privilege and a compatible group state. A lecturer can approve their own submission when they also hold the department's approval privilege.                                       |
| Registration oversight | `read_registration` plus `manage_department` for the department, or university-wide `manage_university` | Reads departmental enrollments for the selected semester.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Delegated registration | `register_student_courses` in the student's department                                                  | Uses the student's saved semester placement, the backend's eligible offerings, and its credit limit. Directory selection additionally requires `read_user`; a staff member with registration permission alone can enter a known student account ID. Closed offerings never accept late registration. |
| Academic setup         | `manage_department` or university-wide `manage_university`                                              | Department managers administer their programmes, curriculum, offerings, and eligible lecturers. University setup, course catalog, and academic level status controls require university management. Faculty registration semester and offering activation use the backend invariants.                |
| Accounts               | `read_user`, `read_admin`, `create_student`, or `create_admin` for the relevant operation               | Lists and opens permitted accounts, provisions students or staff, edits permitted profiles and placement, manages status/sessions, and redacts deleted accounts. Each action has its own privilege check.                                                                                            |
| Role assignments       | `manage_admin_roles` for the chosen scope and every delegated privilege in that scope                   | Assigns and revokes eligible grants. A scoped HOD can provision the seeded Lecturer role during staff creation. Global operations and protected Super Admin assignments follow the API's additional requirements.                                                                                    |
| Roles                  | University-wide `read_role` or `manage_roles`                                                           | Reads definitions and creates, edits, or deletes custom roles within the assigner's delegated privileges. Seeded roles remain protected.                                                                                                                                                             |
| Reports                | `read_reports` for a department, or a university-wide grant for the entire university                   | Shows enrollment and result workflow counts and published performance summaries. A report-only role receives its own authorized filter choices. Draft marks and personal identifiers are excluded.                                                                                                   |
| Audit trail            | University-wide `read_audit`                                                                            | Filters and inspects the append-only audit projection. Safe IDs, outcomes, reasons, and allowlisted changes are read-only.                                                                                                                                                                           |

Privileges from different department grants cannot be combined to authorize an operation in either department. A global grant can supply its privilege to a department operation. Review choices come from `/v1/admin/results/options`; inactive departments remain selectable because deactivation does not delete historical results. The endpoint returns only the departments covered by review, approval, or reopening access and uses one snapshot for authorization and choices.

Approval publishes student grades and recalculates the values returned by result statements. Reopening withdraws the awards and returns the group to draft, while registration stays closed. Student pages, downloads, GPA, and CGPA use the backend's currently approved awards and pinned grading policy. See the [results workflow guide](../../docs/results-workflow.md) for state transitions, score limits, weighted calculations, and repeated attempts.

## UI state and mobile use

Staff resources show loading, empty, restricted, error, and retry states. Mutations show friendly field or action errors. Pending dialogs lock their controls and dismissal to prevent duplicate or ambiguous writes. Denied operations refresh current grants. Teaching and review details expose a refresh action for external changes and state conflicts.

Marks stay mounted when switching between class roster and marks. Both marks pagination and roster pagination are disabled while edits are unsaved, because changing a roster page otherwise remounts the editor. Save or discard edits before paging, refreshing, closing registration, or submitting. Leaving the workspace or reloading the browser discards unsaved edits. Unmatched nested staff URLs show a recovery link to the owning workspace.

Forms and record cards stack on phones. Wide roster, result, report, and directory tables scroll inside labeled regions; the page itself must not overflow. The mobile navigation drawer supports touch, Escape, and focus restoration. Both themes use shared tokens and components.

## Component ownership

`apps/admin/src/app.tsx` composes the shell and route guards. `features/portal/workspaces.ts` supplies both route access and navigation. `features/access/policy.ts` owns frontend scope checks. Auth owns session and authorization revalidation, while `features/portal/staff-data.ts` provides abortable reads, mutation state, and cursor navigation.

Domain UI lives in each feature's `components/` folder: academics, teaching, review, registration, people, roles, reporting, and audit. Field groups, account actions, grant assignment, marks editing, and result rendering are reusable components inside their owning feature. Shared staff resource states, dialogs, record cards, and route recovery live in `features/portal/components/`. Shared presentation primitives stay in `packages/ui`; transport and generated API DTOs stay in their own packages. No package imports application internals.

## Verification

Run `pnpm check` from `frontend/` for formatting, lint, strict application types, unit tests, and both production builds. Run the real-backend browser suite with `pnpm test:e2e` using the disposable demo settings in the [README](../README.md#verification). Never point these mutation tests at an everyday development or production database.

The browser suite covers lecturer marks and submission, HOD correction/publication/reopening, student visibility and weighted GPA changes, delegated enrollment, HOD Lecturer provisioning and scoped profile editing, academic setup, custom role assignment/revocation, report-only access, audit inspection, and denied direct routes. Regression cases exercise a one-row roster page with unsaved marks, an approval-only grant in another department, inactive review scopes, and approval of a lecturer's own submission. Staff pages are checked at 320, 390, 768, 1024, and 1440 CSS pixels and scanned for WCAG accessibility violations in light and dark themes. These automated checks supplement browser testing; they do not replace assistive-technology review.
